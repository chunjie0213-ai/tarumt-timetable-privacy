TAR UMT Assistant Lab

A small test, run by one TAR UMT student, of a Telegram assistant for other TAR UMT students. A student pastes their weekly timetable into the Telegram bot, can connect their own Google Classroom (read-only), and can give an AI assistant of their choice a private, read-only link to their own classes and Classroom deadlines.

Taking part is optional and free. It is a test with a small number of students, not a finished service, and it may stop at any time.
