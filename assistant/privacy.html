TAR UMT Assistant Lab

Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Who runs it

One TAR UMT student (the operator) runs this test on their own computer. It is used only by students who start the Telegram bot themselves.

What it keeps

  • Your Telegram user ID and chat ID, so the bot can reply to you.
  • The weekly classes you paste and confirm: day, time, course code, class type and venue. The pasted text itself is used only to read those classes and is not saved.
  • Your reminder settings.
  • If you connect Google Classroom: an encrypted Google sign-in token, the permissions you granted, and the names and IDs of your Classroom courses with the ones you chose for this semester.
  • If you create an AI link: only a one-way hash of the link's key, when it was made and last used. The operator cannot see your link.

It is stored in a database on the operator's computer. The operator also looks at counts only (for example, how many students finished setup), never at individual students' data.

What it reads from Google

Only if you connect Google Classroom, and only read-only:

  • your course list (classroom.courses.readonly)
  • coursework and your own submission status (classroom.coursework.me.readonly)
  • course announcements (classroom.announcements.readonly)
  • Classwork materials (classroom.courseworkmaterials.readonly)

It does not read Google Drive. It cannot create, change, submit or delete anything in Google Classroom. Assignment titles and due dates are read when you or your AI assistant ask for them and are not stored.

Your AI link

If you create a private link and add it to an AI assistant (for example Claude), that assistant can read your classes and upcoming Classroom deadlines through it. What the assistant does with them is covered by its own provider's terms. You can turn the link off at any time in the settings panel.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Data from Google is used only to show you your own classes and deadlines.

Deleting your data

Tap "删除我的数据" in the settings panel to delete everything above and revoke the Google access. You can also remove Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. All data is deleted when the test ends.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
