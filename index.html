TAR UMT Timetable

A personal tool that sends its owner, a TAR UMT student, a daily class timetable and reminders about tests and deadlines on Telegram.

It reads the owner's own TAR UMT Student Intranet timetable and, with the owner's permission, the owner's Google Classroom courses, coursework and announcements, read-only. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to anyone else.
